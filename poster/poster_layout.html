Introduction

Motivation

Ethereum records every transaction on a public ledger, so we can follow decentralized apps (DApps), from exchanges to games, from launch to decline.

Use keeps growing: DEX apps went from 657K to 1.14M active wallets a month, and news mentions of these apps rose from 79 (2019) to 1,706 (2023). Yet 68% of the apps that took off peaked near month 3 and kept only about 6%.

Our question: Which DApps last?

(1) Describe the market through six questions. (2) Predict success at months 10–12 from an app’s first 3 or 6 months.

Data

On-chain (market size): wallets, transactions and fees from public Ethereum blocks; 544,296 contracts tied to 676 apps.

Off-chain (attention): 87,330 news articles (2019–25) scored for tone and impact; audit, open-source and governance flags.

Conclusions

Findings: So, which DApps last?

  • Those that start big. Early size predicts best, whatever the model; call winners at month 6.
  • Off-chain adds little. Only press before launch is worth collecting.
  • It depends on the market. In slower markets, size’s weight falls from 27% to 19%.

Discussion

Month 3 hits a wall: more data and seven model families stay at 0.54–0.63. The data, not the model, is the limit.

Limitations

  • Ethereum mainnet only; wallets are not people.
  • 63 test successes; results are associations, not causes.
