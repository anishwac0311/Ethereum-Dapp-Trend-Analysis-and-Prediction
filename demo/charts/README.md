# Demo chart data

One CSV per chart table, with the numbers the demo site draws. Generated from `demo/data.js` by `demo/export_charts.py`; rerun it after `build_data.py`. The DragonQuest example on the Method page uses made-up numbers and has no CSV.

| file | chart | used on | source | how |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [01_market_month.csv](01_market_month.csv) | Market strip: wallets, transactions and gas per month | Home | dapp_data_v2/category_month.csv | Sum over the seven categories. Terra = 2022-05, Dencun = 2024-03. |
| [02_q1_wallets_by_category.csv](02_q1_wallets_by_category.csv) | Monthly active wallets by category | Q1 Growth, scenario walks, Pick a timeline | dapp_data_v2/category_month.csv | active_wallets per category. |
| [02_q1_growth_12m_by_category.csv](02_q1_growth_12m_by_category.csv) | 12-month change in wallets by category | Q1 Growth, scenario walks, Pick a timeline | dapp_data_v2/category_month.csv | Wallets vs the same month a year earlier (0.5 = +50%). |
| [02_q1_active_dapps_by_category.csv](02_q1_active_dapps_by_category.csv) | Active DApps by category | Q1 Growth, scenario walks, Pick a timeline | dapp_data_v2/category_month.csv | active_dapps per category. |
| [03_q3_market_concentration.csv](03_q3_market_concentration.csv) | Share of all wallets held by the top DApps | Q3 Concentration | dapp_data_v2/concentration_month.csv (metric = active_wallets, scope = ecosystem) | Shares of all active wallets that month. |
